About AR Love Letters

More than a message.
A place to make memories personal.

AR Love Letters is a creative digital platform designed to help people express emotions through personalized letters, AI-assisted writing, visual customization and shareable experiences.

Start Creating Our Story
Our Story

Expression should feel personal, not difficult.

Finding the right words can sometimes be harder than having the feelings themselves. AR Love Letters was created to make that process easier while still giving every person control over how their message looks, feels and is shared.

A creative platform built around meaningful expression.

What began with the idea of making digital love letters more beautiful has grown into a broader creative system. Users can write their own message, ask AI for help, design a letter from scratch, create a visual memory experience or preserve a more exclusive private message.

The goal is simple: give people different ways to turn emotions, memories and personal stories into something worth sharing.

Creative freedom

Choose between writing manually, using AI assistance or designing your own visual experience.

Made to be shared

Create a personal experience and share it through a unique link with the person it was made for.

User control

Guests and registered users have different workflows, with persistent storage only where the platform feature requires it.

What We Create

Different emotions deserve different experiences.

AR Love Letters provides multiple creation systems instead of forcing every user into one template or writing style.

Classic Love Letters

Write your own message, choose a visual style and generate a shareable digital letter. Guests can create temporary experiences without immediately creating an account.

AI Magic

Give the system details such as your relationship, occasion, emotional tone, language and personal memories to help transform your ideas into a polished romantic letter.

Custom Canvas

Take direct control of the visual composition with customizable text, backgrounds, alignment, animation and other design elements.

Animated Scrapbook

Combine personal photographs, written memories and music into a cinematic digital experience designed around storytelling and emotional reveal.

Premium Vault Experience

A special private letter format designed around limited access, expiration controls, visual themes and optional soundtrack support.

Built Around Personal Stories

From a short heartfelt message to a complete visual memory, every creation system is designed to help users make the experience feel more personal.

Beyond Creation

The letter is only one part of the platform.

Registered users also have access to tools for managing their creations, reviewing activity and getting help when needed.

Personal Dashboard

Review saved creations, manage letters, access notifications, update profile details and control your account experience.

AR Guide

An interactive assistant experience designed to help users understand the platform and navigate available features.

Support Desk

Logged-in users can submit support requests, report problems, request account deletion and track relevant assistance.

Notifications

Important platform updates and relevant information can be delivered directly through the user dashboard.

Account Controls

Users can manage saved content and request permanent account deletion through the available support process.

Continuously Evolving

AR Love Letters is an actively evolving independent platform with new experiences and improvements being developed over time.

Privacy is part of how the platform is designed.

Different features operate differently. Temporary guest workflows are designed around minimal persistent storage, while registered users can deliberately save supported content for access through their personal dashboard.

Guest sharing Temporary guest workflows can use shareable URL-based data rather than requiring every letter to be permanently stored.
Saved creations Persistent features for authenticated users use cloud infrastructure so creations can be managed through the dashboard.
User control Users can manage individual saved content and use the support process for permanent account deletion requests.
Our Mission

Make expressing something meaningful feel easier.

We believe technology should not replace emotion. It should help people communicate it better. AR Love Letters exists to give users creative tools that turn thoughts, memories and feelings into experiences they can truly make their own.
