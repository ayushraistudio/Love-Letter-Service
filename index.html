Frequently Asked Questions

Is this AI Love Letter Generator free?

Yes, our AI Love Letter Generator is completely free to use. You can create, personalize, and share beautiful romantic letters online without any cost.

Can I personalize my love letter?

Absolutely! You can customize your message, choose from multiple beautiful themes, and generate a unique link to send to your special someone.

Is my data secure and private?

Yes, your privacy is our top priority. All user authentication is securely handled by Firebase. Furthermore, your generated love letters are never stored on our servers; the message data is encrypted and passed directly through the secure URL you share.

Does the receiver need an account to read the letter?

No, the receiver does not need an account. They can simply click the unique link you share with them to read your beautifully designed letter instantly.
